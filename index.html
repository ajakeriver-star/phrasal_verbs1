Phrasal Verbs Reference

Type 1

Intransitive (No Direct Object)

Formula: Verb + Particle

Action complete by itself without a target object.

Common List:

Wake up, Go away, Fall down, Stay up, Break up, Sit down, Take off, Calm down.

Type 2

Transitive Separable

Formula: Verb + Obj + Part / Verb + Part + Obj

Rule: If the object is a PRONOUN (it, him, them), it MUST go in the middle!

Common List:

Wake sb up, Take sth off, Calm sb down, Try sth out, Figure sth out, Make sth up, Throw sth away, Pick sth up, Let sb down.

Type 3

Transitive Inseparable

Formula: Verb + Particle + Object

The verb and particle CANNOT be separated under any circumstance.

Common List:

Look into sth, Focus on sth, Believe in sth, Live for sth, Be into sth, Look after sb.

Type 4

Three-Part Transitive (Inseparable)

Formula: Verb + Part 1 + Part 2 + Object

Has two particles. Always requires an object at the end.

Common List:

Come up with sth, Look down on sb, Look up to sb, Run out of sth, Fall out with sb, Go on about sth, Get away with sth.
